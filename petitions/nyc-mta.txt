TITLE
Make NYC subway announcements readable, not just audible

PUBLIC PETITION
https://www.change.org/p/make-nyc-subway-announcements-readable-not-just-audible

LOCATION
New York City, New York, United States

DECISION-MAKERS
MTA Board and New York City Transit leadership

DESCRIPTION
Everyone deserves to understand important train announcements.

Deaf and hard-of-hearing riders should not have to ask a stranger what was said. Train noise can make messages hard to hear for anyone. Clear text can also give travelers learning English another way to follow.

NYC subway cars such as the R211 already have information displays. We ask MTA and NYC Transit to:

- Review which delays, service changes, and emergency announcements also appear as text, by car type.
- Pilot clear, timely text alongside audio, reusing suitable screens where possible.
- Test with disabled riders, use plain language, and explore translations where feasible.
- Publish the results, costs, and a plan for wider coverage.

Keep audio and existing hearing assistance. Give emergency information priority. The goal is less guessing and more independent travel.

This is an independent community proposal. Complete live-announcement text coverage has not been confirmed; we are asking for a review, not claiming every train lacks it.
